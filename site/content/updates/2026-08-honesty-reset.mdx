We are tightening the gap between vision and verifiable execution.

### What we are changing

- **Removed empty authority claims.** No more “backed by veterans from SpaceX / JPL / DARPA / ESA / Blue Origin” without named people and permission.
- **Withdrawn Q4 2027 as a launch commitment.** That date was marketing-aggressive for a pre-hardware program. Earliest *aspirational* flight-class windows are much later — and only if ground gates pass.
- **Budget honesty.** Public ~$500k is framed as a **ground demonstrator / early R&D campaign**, not a full Venus flight price. Optimistic rideshare math on the site is labeled **study only**.
- **Status theater retired.** Telemetry now reads **PRE-HARDWARE**, not “SYSTEMS NOMINAL.” Concept art is labeled concept art.
- **Public progress tracker.** See [/status](/status) and `docs/STATUS.md` for built vs not-yet.
- **Regulatory path notes.** Draft notes in `docs/regulatory-path.md` — research, not filings.
- **Contribution is real infrastructure.** AETHER OS + GitHub issues (L1–L3). Claim work; ship evidence.

### What stays

- The science interest in the ~50–55 km Venus cloud band remains valid.
- Long-term vision (cloud-layer science → habitation tech) remains inspiration — **labeled as vision**.
- Open invitation: engineers, scientists, writers, fabricators — [GitHub](https://github.com/ehreng/Nephelis) and the volunteer form.

**Next engineering milestone:** acid-resistant material coupons + small helium ground float with basic telemetry. That is the work. Everything else waits on evidence.
