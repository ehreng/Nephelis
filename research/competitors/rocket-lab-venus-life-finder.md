# Competitor: Rocket Lab — Venus Life Finder

**Type:** Private Venus atmospheric mission concept / program  
**Relevance:** Closest well-known private peer for cloud-layer science  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-14

## Summary

Rocket Lab / MIT Venus Life Finder (Morning Star 1) is a ~17–20 kg, 40 cm ballistic cone with **one instrument**: an autofluorescence nephelometer (AFN, 440 nm laser through a fused-silica window). Published cloud-layer dwell is ~5 minutes (about 330 s between ~60 and 48 km). Cost claims remain in the multi-million / sub-$10M class.

As of August–September 2026, Rocket Lab’s public launch manifest listed VLF as TBC (no dated rocket/site). Earlier 2023 / Jan 2025 / 2026 window claims have slipped. Treat schedule as uncommitted. Morning Star follow-ons discussed in coverage: ORIGIN laser-desorption mass spec, then a ~5 m balloon for up to about a week.
