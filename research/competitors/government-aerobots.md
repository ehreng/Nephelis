# Government / agency aerobot concepts

**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-14

## NASA DAVINCI (committed Discovery, early 2030s)

Descent sphere, ~55–60 min full-column snapshot. Probe payload: Venus Mass Spectrometer (VMS), Venus Tunable Laser Spectrometer (VTLS), Venus Atmospheric Structure Investigation (VASI: T/P/wind), Venus Descent Imager (VenDI, NIR **surface** imaging). Carrier also flies VISOR and CUVIS. Public 2026 coverage: thermal-vac of the descent sphere; launch/descent dates still moving inside the early 2030s. Cloudseeker does not copy VenDI (we stay at 50–55 km) and does not claim NASA instruments.

## NASA Venus aerobot concepts (study, not a mission)

JPL / Aerostar variable-altitude balloon-in-balloon: outer zero-pressure + inner super-pressure, target 52–62 km, 1 month to 100 Earth-days. ~12 m diameter for ~100 kg science. Earth 6.4 m TRL-5 prototype inflated Feb 2025; Nevada density-equivalent flights 2024. Science discussed: aerosols, met, infrasound. Complementary to Cloudseeker (they would yo-yo altitude; we lock the habitable slab). Do not present as a funded flight.

NIAC EVE (electrolysis ISRU for buoyancy gas / night power) is a concept, not hardware.

## ESA / other

- EnVision: orbiter (VenSAR radar). Maps. Does not float.
- VERITAS: NASA orbiter mapping. Same.
- Historical: Venus Express (orbital atmosphere)
- Conceptual: VEP-class balloon ideas periodically resurface in white papers

## Heritage that matters operationally

- **VEGA 1 & 2 (1985):** only successful Venus balloon flights. 3.54 m Teflon super-pressure, 6.9 kg gondola, ~46 h at ~54 km. Payload: T, P, vertical wind, backscatter nephelometer, ambient light, lightning. Battery-limited. Gold standard for duration/float lessons.
- **Pioneer Venus multiprobe:** atmospheric structure entry profiles
