The program now has a ground-first sequence with KPIs and spend ranges. Unique value is **time at 50–55 km** (30–90 days vs VLF ~5 min / DAVINCI ~1 h / VEGA ~46 h). That lives or dies on the envelope.

Public **$500k** funds Gates 0–5 if spend is tight (comfortably 0–4). Flight-class cost stays **$5–15M+** or a strong partnership. No committed launch date. Earliest class if gates pass: ~2030–2031+.

### Near-term gates

0. **Film RFQ + lab** — blocked on a US ship-to / ground operator.
1. **Acid coupons** — ASTM D543-class H₂SO₄; Pass / Conditional / Fail.
2. **Ø 1.2 m helium float** — Earth ambient; seams, leak rate, packet.
3. **Bay mock + packet** — COTS camera + T/P + ring emitting AETHER-G3/1.

Electronics, flight avionics, and the 3U CubeSat wait until later gates. Site, visual lock, and the sensor/power books are ahead of hardware.
